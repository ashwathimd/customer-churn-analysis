# Customer Retention Analytics

## Business Problem

The telecom company is experiencing a customer churn, which directly imoacts revenue and customer lifetime value. This project aims to identify the key factors associated with churn, quantify their impact, and provide data driven recommmendations that help the company improve customer retention

## Objective

Analyze customer behavior and identify the factors that contribute most to customer churn using Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI. Generate actionable insights and recommendations that help the telecom company improve customer retention and reduce churn.

## Stakeholders
- CEO / Executive Leadership
- Marketing Manager
- Customer Success Manager
- Sales Manager
- Product / Service Manager

## Business Questions

- What is the overall customer churn rate?
- Which customer segments have the highest churn?
- Which services are associated with higher churn?
- Which contract types experience the highest churn?
- Does payment method influence churn?
- How do monthly charges and total charges relate to churn?
- How does customer tenure affect churn?
- Which customer segments should be targeted for retention?
- What recommendations can reduce customer churn?
